# Private rooms: elevation asset notes

Companion to `bedroom.svg`, `bathroom.svg` and `office.svg`. Markers are unique within each wall (for example, `BA-S/3`). The same reusable item may appear in several views. Dimensions are metres and are targets for the next design pass; verify attachment and clearance in the 3D blockout. `[Candidate]` means an animation idea, not a promise of existing gameplay.

## Durable asset registry

IDs are stable design IDs for the next asset stage. Counts refer to visible physical instances in these three rooms, not the number of elevations in which an object appears. Repeated item geometry may share one model. Items embedded in an assembly remain distinct IDs when they require their own concept or texture. IDs and counts are provisional until the plan and elevations are jointly approved.
