Move the sofa in from the perimeter and turn it toward the east wall. Put the chair at the north end of the same rug, facing the coffee table. The west side stays open to the kitchen.

Tradeoff: The sofa back separates the kitchen approach from the lounge; the room has a stronger east–west division.
