# Apartment plan comparison

These are spatial studies, not yet buildable plans. The final candidate needs dimensioned openings, furniture footprints and a circulation check before elevations and models are approved.

| Study | Character and strengths | Problems / tradeoffs |
|---|---|---|
| A — gallery | Compact rectangular footprint, legible public and private rooms, generous kitchen and living room. | Hall needs widening for the camera; closet enclosures, furniture placement and bath window need refinement. Kitchen and bath are across the hall, so plumbing needs a service chase. |
| B — spine | Entry offers a sequence of rooms and a dual-aspect living room. | Deep hall lacks a strong terminating view; bath fixtures and furniture need wall alignment. |
| C — offset | Entry and offset hall offer a more distinctive arrival and varied sightlines. | Bedroom is narrow and accessed from the entry; bath has no window in this study. |
| D — gallery hall | Adjacent kitchen and bath simplify wet services; long views and exterior light. | Much larger footprint and repetitive corridor; furniture, fuse access and circulation need further study. |
| E — T-spine | Dual-aspect living room and an entry-adjacent office. | Office lacks a window, coat closet opens into office rather than hall, and wet rooms are separated. |

**Working selection: A**, subject to revision. It offers the best balance of compactness, room identity and usable space for gameplay. The refinement must widen the hall/entry, provide real accessible closets, align furnishings and fixtures to walls, add a bathroom window, place the fuse accessibly, and resolve a plausible service route. The initial SVG is retained to show the progression. An independent review found that none of the initial studies was ready to use as a construction plan.

The A+ refinement adds a 1.80 m nominal hall, fitted closets, and an exterior bathroom window. The bedroom closet is now 1.80 × 1.45 m nominal, with a single hanging bulb and mold on its inside back wall. The dresser was made shallower to leave a nominal 0.90 m beside the bed. The lead-paint hunk belongs to the white bedroom wainscoting. Final camera, door-swing, trim and capsule checks remain open.
