# Malcolm: Black Mold

**Room:** Bedroom wall · **Glow:** `#6BCF7F` · **Dialogue:** `malcolm.json`

## Role
Love interest (toxic). Nothing positive about him, and he knows it.

## Description & personality
- Pale grey-green, head-to-toe black, beret, long scarf.
- Emo poet: "Banana" (forty pages), "Ode to the Homeowner," a haiku missing a line "because I am also missing something."
- A sub: asks to be told what to do; harsh orders raise romance *and* friction.
- Traumatised by bleach.

## Arc
1. **v1:** Unfolds from the stain. Dramatic / okay? / "Get off my wall."
2. **v2:** Reads you a poem; Lukey codependency (if you know about it).
3. **v3:** "Tell me what to do." Tender order (romance), give him choice (friendship), harsh order (toxic romance), or Lukey option.
4. **v4:** The scrub brush. If Lukey is fixed he's already drying out (`v4_dry`). Endings: *Fungus Among Us* / *Spore Loser* / *Mildew Friends Forever* / *Rot in Peace*.
