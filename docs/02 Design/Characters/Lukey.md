# Lukey: Leaky Plumbing

**Room:** Kitchen (under the sink) · **Glow:** `#4FA8FF` · **Dialogue:** `Content/ICanFixHim/Data/Dialogue/lukey.json`

## Role
Love interest. The flirtiest of the bunch.

## Description & personality
- Big, grizzly, good-natured; red plaid flannel, blue cap, beard, tool belt.
- Installed in 1987 by "Gary, who did NOT read the instructions."
- Lonely after thirty years under a sink, but covers it with jokes.
- Pipe = innuendo; endless wet jokes ("It's a forecast. Ninety percent chance of me.").

## Arc
1. **v1:** Bursts out of the sink. Flirt / ask why he's wet / tell him to get out.
2. **v2:** Backstory (Gary), loneliness, the [secrets] topic reveals Malcolm drinks his leaks → sets `flag.lukey_feeds_mold`.
3. **v3:** "When I'm dry, what are we?" Romance (first kiss), friendship, cold, or the Malcolm option (sets `flag.lukey_will_dry_malcolm`).
4. **v4:** The wrench. Endings: *Head Over Heels Over Pipes* / *Water Under the Bridge* / *Pipe Dreams Come True* / *Drain-ed*.

## Interconnections
- Fixing Lukey dries out [[Malcolm]] (his finale changes to `v4_dry`).
