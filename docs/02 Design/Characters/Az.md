# Az: Asbestos

**Room:** attic? (TBD) · **Dialogue:** not written yet

## Role
Love interest (backlog). A diva. Asbestos stops fire spreading, so under the sparkle he's protective.

## Description & personality
- A K-pop idol: polished, media-trained, sweet to your face and a diva when he doesn't get his way.
- Protective of the house (and you). Being "removed" is his worst nightmare: he's *heritage*.

## Voice
**A K-pop idol: bright, breathy and sweet, with a soft Korean accent.** A light, clear tenor that drops into a husky low register when he flirts; musical, playful sing-song teasing, aegyo-style sulks and diva gasps. Keep the accent natural and affectionate, never a joke. None of the ElevenLabs defaults fit, so use Voice Design or the Voice Library (the stopgap default is **Liam**).
