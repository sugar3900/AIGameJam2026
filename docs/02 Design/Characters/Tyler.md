# Tyler: Pink Tile

**Room:** Bathroom (the 1957 tile wall) · **Glow:** `#FF7AB8` · **Dialogue:** `tyler.json`

## Role
Love interest. Obnoxious, bad taste, outdated about gender, and secretly soft.

## Description & personality
- Salmon blazer energy, pink sweater vest, pompadour, a "Gentleman's Push-Broom" mustache.
- Defends pink as historically masculine ("Blue was for girls. I'm a historian. I have read a placard.").
- "In my day we had two genders and four bathroom colors."
- Terrified of subway tile and modern farmhouse ("It's a hostage situation").

## Arc
1. **v1:** Steps out of the wall. Mustache compliment / "a LOT of pink" (pink history lesson; "colors don't have genders") / "you're hideous."
2. **v2:** Grout pride, Baker-Miller pink, the goth's mildew, Wyatt gossip (`flag.tyler_curious`).
3. **v3:** Found your renovation catalogs. Restore him (romance) / "you can change too" (friendship: *Everybody Pink*) / gut him.
4. **v4:** The grout float. Endings: *Tile Death Do Us Part* / *Grout of Here* / *Pink for Everybody* / *Retiled*.
