# Wyatt: Faulty Wiring

**Room:** Living room fuse box · **Glow:** `#FFD54A` · **Dialogue:** `wyatt.json`

## Role
Love interest. Always a little confused; his wires are crossed.

## Description & personality
- Lanky, static-fluffed glowing yellow hair, orange shirt, red and blue wires slung over his back.
- "Wyatt. Like 'wire it.' Like, wire it wrong." Installed upside down in 1974.
- Bisexual: "very AC/DC. Both currents." Crushes make the lights brown out.
- Lights flicker when he thinks; fires are "starter fires."

## Arc
1. **v1:** Stumbles out looking for the bathroom. Electric flirt / flickering lights / "you set my wall on fire."
2. **v2:** Crushes, "Bi-five!", untangling his cord ("you're very grounding"), Tyler crush if you've met Tyler (`flag.wyatt_likes_tyler`).
3. **v3:** Fear of being boring once he's up to code. Romance / favorite weirdo / safety first / "tell Tyler."
4. **v4:** Wire strippers. Endings: *You Light Up My Life* / *Short Circuit* / *Current Besties* / *Disconnected*.

## Interconnections
- Crush on [[Tyler]]; telling him to confess raises Tyler's romance.

## Voice
**Smart, masculine Australian, a little submissive**: a deep, gentle baritone, thoughtful, trails off politely ("if that's... alright?"), quick and nerdy about circuits, then catches himself. His crossed wires now read as overthinking rather than confusion: smart and flustered, never dim. ElevenLabs fallback: **Charlie**. Full brief and Voice Design prompt: [[Voice Casting]].
