# Scope & MVP

## Elevator Pitch
A girl buys a house thats decrepit and lots of parts need to be fixed, and as she fixes each part, she learns that there are hot male strangers trapped that are the different avatars of the house's maladies.  As she fixes the maladies she gets to know the strangers and optionally develops romantic story lines with them, or friendships.  From flirting to kissing to fade to black (sex).
