# KI-ART-01 — framed kitchen still life

Provisional Blender candidate for the A+ kitchen. A generated multiview concept (`concepts/still_life_multiview.png`) guides the period walnut frame; the separately generated full-bleed painting (`textures/kitchen_still_life.png`) shows a pitcher, fruit and roses in warm window light. The final painting differs from the reference sheet's simplified arrangement and has visible oil-paint brushwork. `textures/walnut_painterly.png` is reused from `HA-ART-09` for consistency. The modeled frame is a scaled adaptation of that reviewed asset's mitered frame, with raised lip, muted brass fillet, canvas, backing and small mounting plate.

- Dimensions: **0.600 m wide × 0.035 m deep × 0.450 m high**. Blender coordinates use metres, X width, Z up, with front facing −Y; rear is Y=0. Geometry occupies Z=0–0.45 m. The FBX is exported with −Y forward, Z up, unit scale; confirm Unreal's import transform and pivot before mounting the rear flush against the wall.
- `model/KI-ART-01.blend` is the editable source, `model/KI-ART-01.fbx` the six-mesh export, and `model/build.py` and `model/validate.py` reproduce and validate them. Reimport reports six meshes, 122 triangles, finite coordinates, UV layers and no zero-area geometry triangles, with 0.600 × 0.035 × 0.450 m bounds. See `model/validation.txt`.
- The picture uses a single coherent canvas UV island with a rough opaque material; the generated full-bleed painting is slightly stretched horizontally to fit the frame opening. Recreate `Still-life painting`, `Aged walnut`, muted brass and backing materials in Unreal and reconnect both sRGB textures. FBX does not guarantee the Blender material graph transfers. Use no collision or a simple thin box if required.
- Placement, material response and readability under final Unreal lighting are not yet tested. This is a static picture; no interaction is implemented.

Generated art prompt: “Richly painterly vintage still life ... cream ceramic pitcher, pears and lemons, faded rose blooms ... warm walnut table ... soft window daylight ... full-bleed 4:3 landscape painting ... no frame, text or signatures.” The multiview prompt requested the same 60 × 45 cm walnut/brass frame from front, back, both sides and perspective, guided by the painterly fixer-upper reference.
