# BR-ART-FLORAL-01 — bedroom botanical print

Original painterly rose-and-wildflower artwork, designed for the A+ bedroom north wall (`P-ART-BR-FLORAL`). It has a period walnut frame, mitered corners, slim brass inner fillet, flat print surface and rear backing/mount. Artwork is static; optional future inspect interaction would be implemented in Unreal, not in this export.

- Model measures **0.450 m wide × 0.035 m deep × 0.550 m high**. Blender uses metres, Z up; X is width, front faces −Y, the rear is at Y=0, and bottom is Z=0. FBX uses −Y forward and Z up. Verify axis and pivot on Unreal import.
- `concepts/botanical-artwork-v1.png` is the original generated portrait artwork; `references/framed-multiview.png` illustrates the front, back, left, right and perspective design. Generated views are guidance; the actual 3D model and dimensions control construction. The modeled rear uses a small simple mounting plate rather than the illustrated hanging loop.
- `textures/botanical_print.png` is the full-bleed print; `textures/walnut_painterly.png` is reused from `HA-ART-09` for visual continuity. Textures are packed in the blend and also supplied externally. Picture material has one UV island and is an opaque rough print; no glass is modeled. Blender shading nodes may not transfer through FBX: reconnect both sRGB color textures to their respective materials, and use muted metallic brass for the fillet in Unreal.
- Source `model/BR-ART-FLORAL-01.blend`, reproducible `model/build.py`, and `model/BR-ART-FLORAL-01.fbx` contain the asset. `previews/art-front.png`, `art-three-quarter.png`, and `art-side.png` show studio renders. The temporary background wall is not in the FBX or saved blend.
- Headless Blender FBX round trip via `model/validate.py` passed: 6 mesh objects, 122 triangles, finite coordinates and UVs, nondegenerate geometry and 0.450 × 0.035 × 0.550 m reimported bounds. Full log is `model/validation.txt`.
- Intended placement is bedroom north wall, above wainscoting and clear of the closet/door opening; coordinate exact placement with the elevation and assembled shell. Use no collision or a simple thin box only if needed. Unreal import, material reconstruction, lighting, collision, and in-room appearance have not been tested.
