# KI-BOWL-01: ceramic fruit bowl

Provisional art candidate for the kitchen table; Unreal import and placement have not been tested. The generated `concepts/multiview.png` establishes the scalloped cream bowl, painted floral branch, two apples, two lemons and one pear. The modeled shape and positions are the authoritative geometry; generated views are visual guidance.

The Blender source is `model/KI-BOWL-01.blend`, and `model/build_bowl.py` rebuilds the source, six FBXs and `previews/bowl-three-quarter.png`. The bowl is a solid, hollow revolved mesh with a small foot and subtly scalloped rim, approximately 0.305 × 0.254 × 0.134 m, Z up, front toward −Y, origin at bowl floor center. It has floral exterior (`ceramic-floral.png`), inner cream glaze (`ceramic.png`) and warm rim material slots. Brush and crazing are color texture, not modeled surface displacement. The front floral design uses a custom radial UV map; recreate the material assignment and sample image color in Unreal. Inspect the texture seam on the back under actual lighting.

`KI_Bowl.fbx` is the bowl. `Apple_A.fbx`, `Apple_B.fbx`, `Pear.fbx`, `Lemon_A.fbx`, and `Lemon_B.fbx` are individually selectable fruit, each with its own origin at its center; apples and pear include modeled stems in their mesh. The source assembly centers relative to the bowl are: Apple A (−0.066, 0.028, 0.100), Apple B (0.067, 0.027, 0.100), pear (0, 0.037, 0.110), lemon A (−0.038, −0.051, 0.090), lemon B (0.041, −0.050, 0.090), in metres. FBX importer transform conventions may differ: verify local locations and scales on import rather than applying these offsets twice. Fruit color materials sample independent `apple.png`, `lemon.png` and `pear.png`; stems use a simple brown material. This is only an animation-ready separation, not implemented pickup behavior.

FBX reimport using `model/validate_fbx.py` reports UV layers, finite coordinates, and nonzero triangle areas on all six exports; see `model/validation.txt`. The fruit caps have convergent sphere UVs, and texture blending at the seams should be assessed in Unreal. Final collision, support/contact positions, material recreation, table placement, color and style remain to be tested in the assembled level.
