# BA-MAT-01 — woven bathroom mat

Provisional Blender asset for the A+ bathroom, pending lead review and Unreal assessment. The selected design is `concepts/bath-mat-v1.png`; `textures/bath-mat-albedo.png` is an original generated top-down painterly cotton color texture derived from that design. It is one full-mat image, not a tiled fabric: the cream loops and dusty rose scalloped border are painted into color, not simulated yarn or raised paint.

The editable source is `model/BA-MAT-01.blend`; `model/BA_Mat.fbx` is the exported mat alone. Preview-only floor, lights and camera are excluded from the FBX. The Blender origin is floor-centered with X along the longer side and Z up. FBX round-trip bounds are approximately **0.552 × 0.402 × 0.0064 m**, including small handmade edge undulations, with 2,146 vertices and 4,288 triangles. Target plan footprint is 0.55 × 0.40 m at global plan `[5.45, 8.25, 0.55, 0.40]`; check tub access and fit after accounting for the actual mesh bounds.

`previews/mat-perspective.png` and `previews/mat-top.png` show the mesh and original texture under studio lighting. Surface detail is color-only; the actual geometry has a thin solidified edge and low-amplitude soft waves. In Unreal, reconnect the albedo texture as sRGB base color, use a high-roughness opaque textile material, and disable blocking collision unless gameplay specifically requires it. Place the mat just above the bathroom floor to avoid z-fighting and check its perimeter and generated texture edge under game lighting. The preview floor and lighting are not exported; no Unreal material, collision, placement or fresh-load test has been performed.

Rebuild from any directory with `/Applications/Blender.app/Contents/MacOS/Blender -b --python <absolute path>/model/build_mat.py`. Validate with Blender `-b --python <absolute path>/model/validate_fbx.py`. Clean FBX reimport verified one mesh, finite geometry/normals/UVs, a UV layer, nonzero triangle area and the dimensions above.
