# BR-CLOSET-01 · Bedroom closet and mold reveal

Status: **prototype; visual and Unreal review required**. Intended for the A+ bedroom, not yet imported into the game.

`concepts/closet-mold-v2.png` is the selected closed/open/repaired target. `references/multiview-v1.png` is generated visual guidance; its illustrated front aperture is not dimensionally reliable. The plan takes precedence: 1.80 × 1.45 m nominal outer footprint, 2.75 m ceiling, 0.90 m entry, two 0.45 m outward swinging leaves. Actual interior clear floor is about 1.64 × 1.29 m before skirting. There is a single bare bulb; its bottom is about 2.06 m above the finished floor. Its appearance in Unreal needs its own point light and/or emissive material.

The floor, interior walls, high side shelves and hangers, framing and bulb are in `model/BR_Closet_Body.fbx`; hinge-pivoted leaves are `BR_Closet_Door_L.fbx` and `BR_Closet_Door_R.fbx`; the separate back-wall patch is `BR_Closet_Mold.fbx`. Hide the mold mesh upon repair, leaving the clean plaster intact. The Blender master retains individual parts and saves in the open, damaged pose for editing; the FBX door meshes are exported from the closed pose. Coordinate system: metres, Z up, X across the doorway, front faces -Y; body origin is floor centre. The two door hinges lie at X = -0.45 and +0.45 m, Y = -0.753 m; assemble at these hinge offsets relative to the body origin and rotate around Z in opposite directions. Confirm FBX/Unreal axis and pivot behavior in-engine before integrating.

Textures are included in `textures/`; preview lighting is illustrative, not final game lighting. Multi-view AI reference is an aid only: reconcile its inconsistent aperture against the approved plan. The mold is a separate UV-mapped alpha-masked painted plane. Recreate its tinted masked material in Unreal: the FBX alone does not carry Blender shader nodes. Inspect it against the wall in Unreal for any rectangular fringe and tune mask/opacity under final lighting. Shelves are intentionally high and shallow, but character, opening and dialogue camera clearances must be checked in the actual game, particularly the 0.90 m doorway and bulb position. World geometry must not duplicate the assembly walls.

Build with `/Applications/Blender.app/Contents/MacOS/Blender -b --python model/build_closet.py` from any working directory. Validate exports with `Blender -b --python model/validate_fbx.py`. Round-trip Blender validation on the current four FBXs reports finite vertices/UVs and zero degenerate faces. Reimport and fresh-load tests in Unreal have **not** been performed.

`previews/interior_oblique.png` shows the bulb cord, high shelving and unobstructed floor. The generated `textures/mold-mask.png` supplies the alpha texture; Blender uses its alpha with a low threshold and tinted color, not a bright rectangular cutout.
