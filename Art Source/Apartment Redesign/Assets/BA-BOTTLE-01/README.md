# BA-BOTTLE-01 — vintage bathroom toiletry bottle

Provisional Blender asset for the bathroom shelf, pending art-lead review and Unreal placement. `concepts/bottle-multiview-v1.png` shows front, back, left, right, perspective and stopper-removed concept states. It and the two independent original material images were generated with the built-in imagegen tool. The concept prompt called for a 10 × 20 cm old opaque muted-sage toiletry bottle, ivory removable stopper, restrained floral detail, no modern branding, and the apartment reference's painterly rendering. `textures/sage-glaze.png` is the muted ceramic color texture; `textures/floral-panel.png` is the separate sage/rose painted panel. Neither texture is a normal or displacement map.

The body is **0.100 × 0.100 × 0.175 m**, with a real open neck, rounded-square shoulder and floor/tabletop-centred origin; +Z is up, front is local −Y. The separately exported ivory stopper has its origin at `(0, 0, 0.175)` m relative to the body; closed overall height is ~0.199 m. `model/BA_Bottle_Body.fbx` and `model/BA_Bottle_Stopper.fbx` are separate; preserve/recreate the stopper's relative assembly offset after FBX axis conversion. A lifted stopper is shown in `previews/bottle-open.png`; no animation or pickup is encoded.

The painted floral panel is assigned directly to the front-facing body polygons and has no separate surface or offset; its image includes a fine decorative border. In Unreal, assign the two sRGB textures to their separate body slots and recreate restrained matte ceramic; Blender nodes do not transfer as an Unreal shader. The floral and plain-sage images differ in value, so their material boundary remains visually legible and needs an in-room review. The ivory stopper is a separate rough ceramic material. Check the wrap seam on the rear, material roughness, contact on the bathroom shelf and exact interaction pivot in engine; these have not been tested in Unreal.

`model/BA-BOTTLE-01.blend` is the editable source. Rebuild with `/Applications/Blender.app/Contents/MacOS/Blender -b --python model/build_bottle.py`. `model/validate_fbx.py` cleanly reimports both FBXs; `model/validation.txt` records geometry/UV finiteness and nonzero geometry and UV triangles. Body: 3,166 triangles; stopper: 1,916. Studio previews are not game screenshots.
