# HA-ART-03 — hallway countryside landscape

Provisional art asset for the A+ hallway. The original generated painting is `textures/countryside.png`: a lavender hillside and path leading to a small stone bridge, distinct from the river-dominant hall focal landscape. `concepts/frame-multiview.png` is a generated frame/construction reference; its scene and hanging-wire details differ from the selected painting and modeled simple mounting plate. The warm painterly rendering is a color/style treatment, not physical paint displacement.

- `model/HA-ART-03.blend` is editable source; `model/HA-ART-03.fbx` is the static artwork assembly. `model/build.py` rebuilds both and the two studio previews; `model/validate.py` reimports the FBX in a clean scene. The build derives from the previously reviewed HA-ART-09 frame, scaled to this plan's dimensions; the walnut texture is copied from that asset for consistency.
- Overall closed dimensions **0.650 × 0.035 × 0.520 m**. Blender space uses X as picture width, Z up, front toward **−Y**, rear at Y=0. The common bottom-centred origin sits at `(0,0,0)` and the picture extends to Z=0.52 m. FBX export uses **−Y forward, Z up** with unit scale; confirm the Unreal import transform and combined pivot before mounting the rear flush to the intended hallway wall.
- Six mesh objects comprise a mitered walnut frame, raised lip, brass inner fillet, flat canvas, backing and rear mount. `textures/countryside.png` and `textures/walnut_painterly.png` are packed in the blend and also available separately. The generated image is fitted to the canvas UV rectangle and slightly stretched horizontally (source ~1.25:1 vs modeled opening ~1.33:1); inspect appearance in the room.
- Unreal must recreate material connections: use the two image maps as sRGB base colors, rough opaque canvas and walnut, and restrained metallic brass. Blender nodes do not guarantee automatic Unreal material reconstruction. Consider no collision or a thin simple collision shape; no interaction is authored.
- The Blender FBX round-trip recorded in `model/validation.txt` reports six meshes, **122 triangles**, bounds **0.650 × 0.035 × 0.520 m**, finite positions and UVs, and no degenerate geometry or UV triangles. Frame-side UV projection was corrected after the first validation exposed collapsed faces. These checks do not establish Unreal material, transform, collision or hallway lighting/readability.

Build and validate from the repository root with `/Applications/Blender.app/Contents/MacOS/Blender -b --python 'Art Source/Apartment Redesign/Assets/HA-ART-03/model/build.py'` and the corresponding `model/validate.py`. `previews/art-front.png` and `previews/art-three-quarter.png` are studio renders, not in-game screenshots.
