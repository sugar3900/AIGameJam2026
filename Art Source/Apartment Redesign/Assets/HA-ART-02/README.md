# HA-ART-02 — hallway family portrait

Provisional art candidate for parent review and subsequent Unreal assessment. A fictional older woman's portrait, not a depiction of a named real person, rendered with warm painterly brushwork and framed in aged walnut with a brass inner fillet. This is a static decorative picture; optional inspection would need game implementation.

- **Dimensions and axes:** 0.380 m wide × 0.035 m deep × 0.420 m high. Blender uses metres, X across, Z up, front facing −Y and rear at Y=0; bottom edge is at Z=0. FBX export uses −Y forward / Z up. Verify converted axes and pivot during Unreal import. Set the rear against the hall wall and use simple or no collision as appropriate.
- **Concept:** `concepts/portrait-artwork-v1.png` was generated with the apartment visual reference, prompting a fictional older woman in a 1930s family portrait, sage dress and lace collar, warm ochre/rose painterly brushwork and no frame or text. `references/framed-multiview.png` depicts front/back/sides and perspective. AI-generated views are guidance only; the measured model governs construction. The backing is modeled with a simple rear mounting plate instead of the illustrated loop.
- **Materials:** `textures/portrait_print.png` is the full-bleed generated print. `textures/walnut_painterly.png` is reused from BR-ART-FLORAL-01 / HA-ART-09 to match existing frame family. The mitered frame has a muted brass inner fillet and separate backing. Textures are packed in the blend and also supplied externally. Reconnect the sRGB color maps and recreate rough wood, opaque print and muted brass in Unreal; Blender shader nodes do not transfer reliably through FBX. No glass is modeled.
- **Files:** `model/HA-ART-02.blend` and `model/build.py` are editable/reproducible sources. `model/HA-ART-02.fbx` is the six-mesh static assembly. `previews/art-front.png`, `art-three-quarter.png` and `art-side.png` show studio views; the preview wall is not exported.
- **Validation:** `model/validate.py` reimports the FBX and verifies finite positions/UVs, nonzero geometry and UV triangle areas and bounds. The latest result in `model/validation.txt` reports six meshes, 122 triangles, bounds `[0.38, 0.035, 0.42]` metres. Side-face UVs were corrected after the initial round trip revealed collapsed islands.
- **Unreal remains untested:** material reconstruction, import axis/pivot conversion, frame position, lighting and hall readability must be checked in the assembled scene.
