# HA-CABINET-01 — shallow hall-end storage cabinet

A low vintage walnut cabinet designed for the A+ gallery hall west end, with a shallow sliding drawer and two independently hinged doors opening onto a shelf. `concepts/cabinet-multiview.png` supplies the generated open-drawer and front/back/side/perspective reference. The generated visual reference is not dimensionally exact: use the source and these dimensions for placement.

Reimported closed assembly bounds are **1.020 × 0.2475 × 0.7195 m**, including hardware, within the 0.25 m depth target. The longer axis follows the hall end wall at the plan's x≈0.06–0.31, y≈4.18–5.20 m. Check physical trim and wall placement rather than treating these nominal coordinates as collision clearances.

`textures/walnut-muted-b.png` is an independently generated walnut candidate from `LV-CHEST-01`, reused for stylistic continuity; the Blender material adjusts saturation to 0.68 and value to 1.35. `walnut-b.png` preserves the earlier hall-console candidate. Roughness is 0.84 and hardware uses an aged brass material. The painted wood texture is color detail, not physical damage. FBX does not transfer Blender shader nodes automatically; recreate the HSV adjustment, wood and brass in Unreal and assess under final lighting.

The body and drawer FBXs use a common floor-centred origin, Z up and front −Y. The drawer preview uses **0.13 m travel toward −Y**; it extends approximately 0.13 m beyond the closed cabinet, and the shallow box is 0.166 m deep. Door leaves export individually at their respective hinges (−0.465 and +0.465 m X, −0.110 m Y, z=0); the open preview rotates them around Z in opposite directions, 75°. The 0.455 m-wide leaves can occupy substantially more hall space when open than the closed cabinet. Test the player's capsule and camera, drawer and door swings, and accessibility in the actual hall before making them interactive; consider restricting door swing where required.

`model/HA-CABINET-01.blend` is editable source. `model/build_cabinet.py` rebuilds source, four FBXs and closed/open renders. `model/validate_fbx.py` reimports every FBX and checks finite vertices, normals, UVs and zero-area polygons/triangles. `model/check_assembly.py` verifies reimported hinge origins and combined closed bounds. Both checks pass in Blender 5.2.3. Blender renders are studio previews. Unreal importer axis conversion, material setup, collision, motion and real room fit are **not yet tested**.
