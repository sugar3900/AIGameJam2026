# LV-PLANT-01 — trailing variegated pothos

A painterly terracotta pot with a muted cream shoulder line and a looser variegated pothos canopy of rounded, lightly curved heart-shaped leaves. Seven individually modeled vines trail over the rim; smoothly curved stems connect the canopy and lower leaves. `concepts/pothos-concept-v1.png` is the design reference; `references/pothos-multiview.png` is a generated directional modeling guide (the views are approximate, not dimensionally authoritative). The modeled leaves use actual heart-shaped geometry and do not require alpha masking.

- Source: `model/LV-PLANT-01.blend`, reproducible with `blender -b --python model/build_plant.py`. Static export: `model/LV_Plant.fbx`.
- Bounds after a fresh FBX reimport: **0.346 × 0.344 × 0.417 m**, including foliage. Pot base is at approximately Z = 0; vines reach Z = −0.030 m, so place it on a stand/shelf or check floor intersection. The assembly is wider and shorter than the preliminary inventory target (0.32 × 0.26 × 0.62 m); confirm composition and clearance at placement rather than assuming it fits.
- UV-mapped generated base-color sources: `textures/terracotta.png` and `textures/pothos-leaf.png`. Both are painterly and visually chosen for the concept; neither is verified to tile seamlessly. Soil, stems and cream ornament use simple material colors. A single exported mesh is provided per material group (five static meshes total). Leaf geometry is one-sided: enable **Two Sided** for the leaf material in Unreal and inspect its normals/lighting from both sides. No alpha mask is needed.
- The FBX does not transfer Blender shader settings; recreate the clay and leaf materials in Unreal and tune roughness and color under game lighting. Test collision on the pot, not the foliage, and test vine intersections with the stand. Import, materials, lighting and in-room appearance have **not** been tested in Unreal.
- `previews/plant-three-quarter.png` is a transparent studio render, not an in-game screenshot. Blender FBX reimport validation (`blender -b --python model/validate_fbx.py`) reports 5 meshes, 16,217 vertices, 27,152 triangles, UVs present, no nonfinite coordinates or zero-area polygons.
