# LV-ART-04 — living-room garden landscape

Static framed artwork for the living-room west wall above the sofa in the A+ plan. `concepts/garden-landscape-v1.png` is the selected frame/painting concept. `textures/garden_landscape.png` is a separately generated full-bleed painting based on that concept, with no baked frame. `textures/walnut_painterly.png` is reused from HA-ART-09 to coordinate the period frames. The modeled asset includes a mitered walnut frame, raised lip, brass inner fillet, canvas, backing and small mounting plate.

- Dimensions: **0.720 m wide × 0.035 m deep × 0.520 m high**. Blender coordinates: X width, Z up, front faces −Y, back is at Y=0; geometry spans Z=0–0.52 m. FBX exported −Y forward, Z up, with unit scale. Confirm Unreal import axes and combined pivot before placement; set the rear of the frame flush with the wall.
- Source: `model/LV-ART-04.blend`, reproducible `model/build.py` and `model/LV-ART-04.fbx`; studio renders are `previews/art-front.png`, `art-three-quarter.png` and `art-side.png`. Generated textures are packed in the blend and supplied separately. The painting is UV mapped as one coherent image; its generated 1.4:1 aspect is close to the 0.618 × 0.418 m exposed canvas (about 1.48:1), so minor proportional change is possible.
- In Unreal reconnect `garden_landscape.png` and `walnut_painterly.png` as sRGB textures to the named materials. Use rough opaque canvas, muted metallic brass and rough wood; Blender shader nodes are not automatically recreated through FBX. No glass is modeled. Use no collision, or a simple thin box if an inspection interaction needs it. Placement, material, collision and appearance under game lighting have not been tested in Unreal.
- Build using `/Applications/Blender.app/Contents/MacOS/Blender -b --python model/build.py` and validate with `Blender -b --python model/validate.py`. FBX reimport validates 6 meshes, 122 triangles, finite vertices and UVs, no zero-area geometry or UV triangles, and the dimensions above.
