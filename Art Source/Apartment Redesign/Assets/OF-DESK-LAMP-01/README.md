# OF-DESK-LAMP-01 — vintage adjustable desk lamp

A small sage enamel and aged brass desk lamp for the office, with a toggle, restrained cord, hollow shade, pivot knobs, and separate bulb. `references/multiview-on-off.png` supplies the character and lighting direction. The model is a fixed-pose interpretation; the arm hinges are visual details and are not rigged. `previews/lamp-off.png` and `lamp-on.png` are fixed-studio Blender renders, not game screenshots.

Three independent enamel concepts are retained under `textures/sage-candidate-*.png`. Candidate A was too bright/green, B too uniformly beige, and selected C (`sage.png`) gives the muted sage, gentle brushwork and small age marks. The originally reused brass read too orange, so two independent muted patina candidates were generated: A was selected for its olive-gold brass and soft green age, while B read excessively dark. The source uses metallic 0.65 and roughness 0.48 for this material; reproduce and tune the look in Unreal. Paint strokes are color information, not physical impasto. The visual development images are references rather than dimensionally consistent blueprints.

The three FBXs — `OF_DeskLamp_Body`, `OF_DeskLamp_Shade` and `OF_DeskLamp_Bulb` — share a table-plane origin, so assemble at the same transform. Overall bounds including cord are approximately 0.44 m wide, 0.18 m deep, and 0.38 m tall. Blender source and build script are under `model/`. Run `Blender --background --python .../model/build_lamp.py` from the worktree; export is selected meshes only. `model/validate_fbx.py` reimports each FBX into a clean scene and checks finite vertices/normals/UVs and positive triangle area; its recorded results are in `validation.txt`.

For Unreal, recreate the brass, sage and ivory materials using the supplied textures, import the three components at the common origin, and use an emissive material instance on the bulb plus a warm point or spot light under the shade for the on state. Tie both to the existing light interaction; the Blender preview light is not included in the FBX. The lamp arm and toggle are fixed visuals, not separately animated parts. Verify texture seam, bulb position, light intensity and shadowing, collision and desk clearance under final exposure in Unreal; none of these have been tested in game.
