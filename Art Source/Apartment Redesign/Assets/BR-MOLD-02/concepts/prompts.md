# Fresh mold texture exploration

Built-in image generation, 2026-11-09, transparent background. Both original generated PNG files are preserved unchanged.

## A — broad colony wash

Flat orthographic black mold decal, transparent background, top-right corner origin. Spread irregular charcoal-black colonies left along the top and down the right, with disconnected small colonies and diffuse stippled edges fading toward the lower left. Painterly dry-brush pigment, subtle warm neutral gray staining, porous charcoal instead of large solid masses. No green, olive, yellow, triangular central blob, raised lumps, lighting, glossy surface, shadow or text.

Result: useful broad distribution but too much pale backing wash; rejected in favour of B.

## B — selected, open corner distribution

Flat transparent mold decal. Only charcoal-black growth marks; every speckle gap transparent. Growth at top right, broken patches left along top, irregularly downward along right. Broad approximately L-shaped organic distribution, lower-left clear. Disconnected dusty colonies and fine satellites around larger fuzzy islands. Porous brush stipple, dark neutral charcoal, no green. Painterly delicate dry-brush edges, variable translucency, transparency inside clusters. Not ink splash, smoke, triangular blob, tendrils, drips, room perspective, lighting, shadow or text.

Result: more open corner spread and neutral pigment. Selected unchanged as the RGBA texture. The material uses both generated alpha and pigment darkness to suppress its pale wash, then supplies a constant neutral charcoal BaseColor. This gives separated colonies and genuine wall gaps under the closet's warm lighting.
