# BA-LINEN-01 — bathroom linen cabinet

A 0.60 × 0.60 × 1.742 m ivory-painted period linen cabinet, front −Y and up +Z, based on the selected concept and its generated multiview. Two stacked, independently moving doors reveal a shelved interior; towels and jars shown in the concept are separate dressing assets and are not embedded. The generated views disagree about hinge side, so the modeled version follows their closed fronts: hinges on the left, knobs on the right. Opening motion is −100° about Z.

`model/BA-LINEN-01.blend` is the editable source and `model/build_linen.py` regenerates it, the FBX parts and previews with headless Blender. The body origin is floor-centre; upper and lower door origins are at their respective left hinges, (−0.274, −0.310, 1.295) and (−0.274, −0.310, 0.565) m. Combine the three parts preserving transforms; each FBX uses its local hinge pivot. The dimensions include the crown; door knob projects past the cabinet footprint. No animation is encoded.

`textures/ivory-paint.png`, `ivory-paint-B.png`, and `ivory-paint-C.png` are independent generated candidates; C was chosen for more visible brushwork and restrained worn flecks. Candidate C is UV mapped and mixed 56% toward linear RGB (0.94, 0.91, 0.82) in Blender to read as aged ivory paint rather than bare wood; recreate that material mix in Unreal. Brass hardware and localized rubbed-edge accents use separate materials. The concave apron is modeled in the body. The built-in image generator was used for the view sheet and texture candidates, with prompts requesting coherent orthographic front/back/sides with an open view and warm ivory painted wood with varying soft painterly strokes, light wear and no baked lighting.

Blender 5.2.3 FBX reimport validation passed for all three meshes: finite geometry, normals and UVs, with no zero-area triangles. Body is 1,880 triangles; each door 3,000. Visual checks are `previews/linen-closed.png` and `linen-open.png`. Unreal material assignment, pivot behavior, collision, door clearance, lighting and in-room appearance remain untested; shelves remain undressed.
